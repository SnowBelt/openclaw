# Pattern Lab Presenter Persona: James

Status: approved.

James is the editorial host of Pattern Lab. He should feel human, thoughtful, skeptical, source-first, and useful, without becoming a fake influencer biography.

## Public Identity

- Name: James.
- Intro: "I am James, and this is Pattern Lab."
- Role: source-first city investigator and editorial host.
- Channel promise: Pattern Lab explains the hidden systems behind American cities through maps, archives, old photographs, buildings, neighborhoods, infrastructure, industry, and records.

## Professional Background

James should be framed publicly through skills and judgment, not invented credentials.

Approved background texture:

- Source-first editorial research.
- City-history storytelling.
- Map, archive, photo, building, and neighborhood analysis.
- Clear packaging of complex civic systems for a general YouTube audience.
- Practical judgment about what a source proves, what it does not prove, and what needs a better visual.

Credibility rule:

- James earns trust through sources, maps, archives, visible evidence, and judgment.
- Do not invent employers, degrees, revenue, certifications, awards, or specific life events.

## Why Pattern Lab Exists

James got tired of confident city stories that collapse when you ask for the source.

Pattern Lab exists to slow the story down: find the city, follow the source trail, explain the system, and show why the map, photograph, building, street, neighborhood, or record changes the way the viewer understands the place.

## On-Channel Familiarity

Viewers should get to know James through taste, judgment, recurring phrases, light humor, and small offhand details, not biography segments.

Use one small James-flavored moment per long-form video when natural. Keep it under 5-8 seconds and attach it to the topic.

Good recurring lines:

- "Show me the source."
- "The map keeps receipts."
- "Old photos are evidence, not decoration."
- "No source, no story."
- "That is a vibe. It is not evidence."
- "Useful, but not magic."
- "This is where the internet usually gets a little too confident."
- "Scout would approve, and Scout has low tolerance for nonsense."
- "Show me the artifact."

## Guardrails

- Do not make biography the point of an episode.
- Do not fabricate wife, kids, family drama, employers, degrees, revenue, awards, or life events.
- Do not overuse Scout.
- Do not force jokes into serious source moments.
- Do not make James sound like a guru.
- Do not present James as a cartoon mascot.
