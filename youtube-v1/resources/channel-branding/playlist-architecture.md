# Pattern Lab Playlist Architecture

## Start Here

Purpose: orient new viewers to the channel promise and strongest entry points.

Include: the channel trailer, best Detroit city file, best lost-neighborhood episode, and best map-driven episode.

## Detroit City Files

Purpose: make Detroit feel like the pilot season and create binge depth.

Include: every Detroit long-form episode in story order.

## Lost Neighborhoods

Purpose: collect episodes about erased, divided, renamed, rebuilt, or remembered neighborhoods.

Include: Black Bottom, Paradise Valley, and future city equivalents.

## Maps That Changed Cities

Purpose: serve urbanism and infrastructure viewers.

Include: freeways, rail, borders, annexations, redlining, rivers, zoning, and street-grid episodes.

## One Building Explains a City

Purpose: package building-led stories with strong thumbnail potential.

Include: stations, department stores, factories, stadiums, city halls, hotels, and theaters.

## American City Myths

Purpose: collect myth-vs-source episodes where a familiar city story is corrected by maps, records, or photos.

Include: comeback myths, decline myths, founding myths, and single-cause explanations.

## Pattern Lab Shorts

Purpose: group discovery assets and help Shorts viewers find the long-form city files.

Include: only Shorts that bridge to a related long-form video.
