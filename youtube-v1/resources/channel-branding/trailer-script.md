Detroit did not simply decline. Chicago did not grow by accident. New Orleans is not just a postcard. American cities leave evidence behind: maps, old photographs, buildings, neighborhoods, borders, highways, factories, music, and the streets people still argue about.

No fake archival photos. No random trivia. No recycled history summaries.

Just the source, the place, and the system behind the story.
