# Pattern Lab Performance Learning Loop

Use this loop after each private/unlisted approval and after each public publish.

## Metrics To Track

- Views: demand signal.
- Impressions: packaging opportunity size.
- CTR: title-thumbnail promise strength.
- Average view duration: pacing and payoff.
- Average percentage viewed: full-video retention quality.
- First 30s retention: hook and visible-source strength.
- 50% retention: middle-section durability.
- Subscribers gained: audience-channel fit.
- Subscribers per 1,000 views: city-file conversion strength.
- Returning viewers: channel promise durability.
- Estimated revenue and RPM: monetization quality once available.
- Shorts viewed vs swiped away: first-frame and hook strength.
- Shorts related-video clicks: Short-to-long conversion.
- City requests: demand for the next city or neighborhood.
- Local corrections: factual or context corrections from people with place knowledge.
- Source suggestions: viewer-provided maps, archives, books, photos, or records to investigate.
- Nostalgia and local emotional comments: signs that the topic connects to real memory.
- Geography confusion: places, boundaries, routes, or neighborhoods that need clearer visuals.
- Source disputes: claims that need review before the topic expands.

## Decision Labels

- double_down: make the next adjacent video because source proof, retention, and audience fit are strong.
- repackage: keep the topic but change title, thumbnail, or opening promise.
- revise_hook: keep the video idea but rebuild the first 30 seconds.
- improve_visual_pacing: keep the topic and hook, but increase map/photo/source movement and reduce static sections.
- retire_topic: stop the lane unless a later trend changes the opportunity.
- expand_into_series: turn a proven city-history pattern into a repeatable series after multiple strong reads.
- spin_off_city_series: build a dedicated sequence for a city, neighborhood, map system, building type, or source-led theme.

## Cadence

- 24 hours after publish: record first read, especially CTR, 30s retention, Shorts viewed-vs-swiped, related-video clicks, city requests, and local corrections.
- 72 hours after publish: compare browse/suggested/search traffic and subscriber conversion.
- 7 days after publish: record durable read, decision label, and next action.
- 30 days after publish: record revenue/RPM and whether the topic continues earning.

## Operating Targets

- Build capacity for 3 high-quality long-form videos per week and 2-3 Shorts per long-form.
- Publish only when source, rights, visual, packaging, and owner-review gates stay green.
- Long-form review target: 40% average viewed.
- First 30-second retention target: 65%+.
- Shorts review target: 60% viewed rate and 80% average percentage viewed.
- Subscriber conversion target: 5-15 subscribers per 1,000 views.
- Low CTR with good retention means repackage.
- Good CTR with weak first-30-second retention means revise the hook.
- Weak retention and weak subscriber conversion means retire or narrow the topic.
