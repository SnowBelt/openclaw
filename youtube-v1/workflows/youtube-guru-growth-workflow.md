# YouTube Guru Growth Workflow

This workflow converts approved YouTube growth expert recommendations into Pattern Lab production gates. It is mandatory for every active Pattern Lab city file.

## Formula

**One city. One strange visual clue. One source trail. One hidden system.**

## Required Gates

1. **Outlier topic mining** — choose topics because similar packaging, topic demand, or benchmark-channel outliers show viewer appetite. Record the viewer-demand reason, proof object, and why the idea can beat generic city-history packaging.
2. **Title/thumbnail testing discipline** — prepare at least three title/thumbnail test pairs. Use `watch_time_share_first_then_ctr` as the winner metric. Do not test misleading promises.
3. **Viewer-avatar topic filter** — write the Morgan-style viewer question and curiosity trigger. Reject topics that are merely historically interesting without a viewer mystery.
4. **Packaging lock before scriptwriting** — lock title, thumbnail hypothesis, first hook, proof object, first-30-second payoff, and audience promise before script approval.
5. **First-30-seconds mini-product** — the opening must include visual clue, contradiction, source proof, stakes, and title-thumbnail payoff by 30 seconds.
6. **Retention boredom-cut pass** — remove repeated points, slow setup, unsupported tangents, and visuals that do not advance curiosity, proof, or payoff.
7. **Thumbnail pre-score** — score all three candidates for phone readability, visual mystery, city anchor, proof object, emotion, and payoff match. The selected candidate must pass the score threshold.
8. **Shorts discovery funnel** — maintain a 5-7 Short concept pack. Every Short must be a standalone clue with hook, visual clue, proof/payoff, comment prompt, and long-form bridge.
9. **Audience satisfaction tracking** — track `I never knew this`, city requests, local corrections, source disputes, confusion, praise for visuals, and expectation mismatch.
10. **Sustainable production governor** — build for 3 long-form videos/week, but publish only when all quality gates pass. Quality beats arbitrary frequency.

## Owner And YouTube Boundary

These gates do not authorize public publishing, YouTube Studio test changes, thumbnail replacement, or new API scopes. Public publish remains a fresh owner approval gate.
