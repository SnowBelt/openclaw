# Pattern Lab Monetization Growth Loop

Use this loop for every public-facing city-history package.

## 1. Topic Economics Gate

- Score the topic before production.
- Reject anything below 80/100.
- Reject any topic that cannot show a real source trail: map, archive photo, record, building, neighborhood, timeline, or then/now evidence.
- Prefer city-history topics with strong local/diaspora interest, broad American-history relevance, visual mystery, advertiser-safe framing, and clear sponsor/affiliate fit.
- Build for 3 high-quality long-form videos per week, but publish only when quality gates stay green.

## 2. Source, Originality, And Policy Gate

- Require one central city artifact: source ledger, map overlay, historical photo board, timeline, building record, neighborhood outline, route map, or myth-vs-source board.
- Keep rights-ledger rows for every historical image, archival video, modern stock clip, generated graphic, voiceover, proof clip, long-form draft, and Short.
- Allow high-quality free stock video only as rights-logged context footage. It can improve pacing and retention, but it cannot replace the source artifact or carry a historical claim.
- Record the synthetic or altered content decision before upload.
- Avoid copied titles, copied thumbnails, reused clips, watermarked previews, editorial-only stock clips, random image-search assets, fake archival visuals, and mass-produced template feel.

## 3. Packaging Gate

- Follow the required autonomous sequence: `OpenClaw strategy/source safety → Canva plugin render → OpenClaw validation → owner review / YouTube test`.
- Generate five title options using city + contradiction + visual evidence.
- Generate three thumbnail candidates from `youtube-v1/resources/thumbnail-click-policy.json`: emotional mystery, map/system proof, and contrarian history angle.
- Build each candidate from one dominant real photo/map/document, one emotional mystery, one city anchor, one proof object, and 2-4 words.
- Prefer people, workers, crowds, transit, factories, street life, attractions, landmarks, vanished places, or strong route/map proof over empty skylines or source-board layouts.
- Reject thumbnails that look like research boards: too many boxes/lines, tiny labels, weak contrast, or more than one competing focal point.
- Select one default title-thumbnail pairing.
- Generate description, tags, chapters, pinned comment, source/attribution section, and Shorts metadata.
- Confirm the title and thumbnail promise match the visible source proof in the first 30 seconds.
- Preserve all three candidates for YouTube A/B testing and judge winners by watch time first, then CTR and retention.

## 4. Retention Ladder Gate

- Open with a visual mystery, contradiction, map, source, or old-photo clue in the first 5 seconds.
- Show source proof in the first 20 seconds.
- Add a new narrative or visual beat every 45-60 seconds.
- Use visuals only when they match narration, source, place, time period, proof state, or payoff.
- End with the consistent city-file outro: `That is the pattern: city, source, system.`

## 5. Owner Approval Gate

- Review in Discord or dashboard.
- Use structured actions: approve, reject, regenerate, repair, revise hook, kill topic, approve private upload, approve public publish.
- Public publish is never automatic.
- Owner approval must cover factual sensitivity, title-thumbnail honesty, historical image rights, AI disclosure, and contested-history handling.

## 6. Public Launch Gate

- Publish long-form first after private checks and owner approval.
- Publish 2-3 Shorts after the long-form URL exists.
- Set each Short's Related Video to the long-form video.
- Bridge Shorts viewers with pinned comments and a light subscribe cue.

## 7. Performance Learning Gate

- Record 24-hour, 72-hour, 7-day, and 30-day metrics.
- Label the result: double_down, repackage, revise_hook, improve_visual_pacing, retire_topic, expand_into_series, spin_off_city_series.
- Feed city requests, local corrections, source disputes, and subscriber-conversion signals into the next topic slate.
