# Pattern Lab Image And Source Prompts: Video 03

Create a city-history visual pack for Detroit. Use verified historical images whenever rights are clear. Do not scrape random image search results. Do not present AI reconstructions as archival photographs.

Before sourcing final images or video, follow the canonical source policy: `youtube-v1/resources/source-media-policy.json`.

Before designing final thumbnails, follow the canonical thumbnail click policy: `youtube-v1/resources/thumbnail-click-policy.json`.

Before rendering through Canva or local fallback, follow the autonomous architecture: `youtube-v1/workflows/autonomous-production-architecture.md`.

Required autonomous sequence: OpenClaw strategy/source safety -> Canva plugin render -> OpenClaw validation -> owner review / YouTube test. Canva is a rendering engine only; OpenClaw owns source safety, title-thumbnail promise matching, rights ledger checks, and validation. Canva Free output must have no watermark and no Pro-locked elements.

Preferred free/low-cost source classes:

- Library of Congress public domain or no-known-restrictions items.
- National Archives public domain federal records.
- Wikimedia Commons files with commercial-use-compatible licenses and attribution.
- Detroit Public Library / Burton Historical Collection items only when reuse rights are clear.
- Detroit Historical Society for research and topic verification; use images only when rights are explicit.
- Pexels, Pixabay, and Unsplash for modern non-proof photo backgrounds or texture only.
- Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, and Coverr for rights-logged modern context B-roll only.
- Videvo/Videezy only when item-level license, attribution, and editorial-use status are logged.
- NASA or Internet Archive/Prelinger archival video only when the item-level rights basis is clear.

First 30-second payoff rule: the first 30 seconds of the video must show the exact map, source, place, or clue promised by the thumbnail. No fake archival photos, no fake source proof, no watermark, and no Pro-locked Canva Free export.

Required thumbnail upgrade:

- City name dominance: DETROIT must be the primary or co-primary text element, never a small corner badge. The city name must stay readable at phone size and in a thumbnail search shelf.
- Thumbnail search shelf: render a repo-local YouTube-style thumbnail search shelf with all five concepts and fail if the city name is not readable, the contrast is weaker than benchmark search examples, or the promise is unclear without reading the title.
- Competitive packaging: use yellow/white city text, one vivid accent color, thick stroke/shadow, darker edges, brighter focal point, one dominant real photo/map/document, and one major proof mark. No source-board clutter, no tiny unreadable labels, no multi-box research-board look.

Required five review concepts:

- DETROIT WAS REWIRED: city + contradiction thumbnail, emotional mystery, one dominant real photo, explicit city anchor, explicit proof object, one rewiring/route proof mark, high contrast, no source-board clutter.
- DETROIT'S HIDDEN MAP: city + map/system proof thumbnail, one dominant real photo/map/route/source artifact, explicit city anchor, explicit proof object, one bold route/arrow/circle proof mark, clear city-system tension, no source-board clutter.
- DETROIT 1942: city + year/time-travel thumbnail, one dominant dated source photo, explicit city anchor, explicit proof object, one dated clue/proof mark, clear reason to click, no tiny source labels.
- DETROIT VANISHED: city + vanished-place thumbnail, one dominant real photo, one vanished-place or before/after clue, explicit city anchor, explicit proof object, one proof mark, credible documentary feel, no source-board clutter.
- NOT JUST DECLINE: documentary fall/rise thumbnail, familiar Detroit story challenged by one source/map/photo clue, large DETROIT city lockup even when the headline omits the city, explicit proof object, clear open loop, readable at phone size, no source-board clutter. Use a 2-4 word thumbnail text block.
