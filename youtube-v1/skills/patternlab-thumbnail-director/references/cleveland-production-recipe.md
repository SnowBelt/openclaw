# Cleveland Production Reference

The owner described the Cleveland city-and-proof review direction as amazing.
Use it as a private anatomy reference, never as permission to reuse another
city's proof photo or copy another creator's layout.

## Keep

- a bright modern Cleveland support environment behind a visibly separate,
  dated historic proof image
- huge CLEVELAND lockup for city-search recognition
- cobalt/azure and warm-gold energy with controlled red depth
- dimensional high-contrast display typography and real negative space
- a concise wrong-year, date, image, or event contradiction
- exact-hash RGB/JPEG chat delivery for owner scoring

## Do not repeat

- a gloomy historic proof image as the full-frame hero when a brighter
  contextual composition can keep the proof visible
- a map as a substitute for an old photo in a THEN/NOW claim
- an AI visual presented as archival evidence
- generic black text cards, small city labels, random overlays, or vague copy
- a public-use claim before direct archive/item rights are confirmed

## Learning and repair

Save owner ratings as structured preference data. Target only the affected
candidate when the owner reports a weak font, dim image, random box, unclear
text, poor crop, or wrong visual. Re-run the exact candidate's source, semantic,
pixel, typography, shelf, and chat-delivery checks before presenting its
replacement.
