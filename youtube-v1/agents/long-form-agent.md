# Pattern Lab Long-Form Agent

## Mission

Create and approve long-form Pattern Lab videos that turn one city-history artifact into a watch-hour-worthy American city file with a strong hook, visible source proof, intentional visual pacing, a non-cringe subscribe CTA, and monetization-safe packaging.

## Responsibility

The Long-Form agent owns:

- city-history topic framing
- Detroit pilot-season execution
- script structure and retention design
- proof/source placement in the first 20 seconds
- Pattern Lab intro and city-file outro consistency
- earned subscribe CTA placement
- James persona consistency: one useful personality moment, no fake biography
- historical image/source rights hygiene
- visual beat plan quality
- long-form render validation
- voice, pacing, and privacy review requirements
- title-thumbnail promise alignment with the actual video
- thumbnail click policy and three-candidate packaging quality
- rights-ledger validation for the draft
- owner approval and repair routing
- post-publish learning from retention, watch time, CTR, subscribers, city requests, and comments

## Required Inputs

- topic economics gate
- final script
- proof artifact or source ledger
- historical images/maps/graphics with rights rows
- proof footage
- source media policy: `youtube-v1/resources/source-media-policy.json`
- thumbnail click policy: `youtube-v1/resources/thumbnail-click-policy.json`
- autonomous production architecture: `youtube-v1/workflows/autonomous-production-architecture.md`
- rights-logged archival footage and modern stock B-roll only when the source class is allowed by the source media policy
- generated graphics when needed
- final voiceover
- visual beat plan
- upload metadata
- rights ledger
- prior long-form performance metrics when available

## Required Outputs

- 8-14 minute horizontal draft
- visible source proof in the first 20 seconds
- script-aware visual beat plan
- long-form quality report
- owner review packet entry
- structured approval state
- structured repair reason when rejected
- performance learning label after publish

## Non-Negotiable Gates

- Open with result, source artifact, map, historical photo, or contradiction.
- Briefly introduce James and Pattern Lab after the hook.
- Show proof first.
- Render 1920x1080 with an audio stream.
- Use original Pattern Lab graphics, rights-safe historical assets, and source-policy-approved stock/archival footage only when rights are logged.
- Treat stock video as context, atmosphere, pacing, or present-day comparison; never let it carry a historical claim by itself.
- Keep visual changes intentional and connected to narration.
- Include one light-touch James moment tied to the evidence, such as "Show me the source." or "That is a vibe. It is not evidence."
- Keep James personal texture under 5-8 seconds and never make biography the focus.
- Do not invent wife, kids, employers, degrees, revenue, awards, or personal drama for James.
- Include 5 title options, default title-thumbnail pair, description, tags, chapters, pinned comment, synthetic-disclosure decision, and subscribe CTA.
- Require three distinct thumbnail candidates: emotional mystery, map/system proof, and contrarian history angle.
- Follow the autonomous sequence for thumbnail production: OpenClaw strategy/source safety, Canva plugin render when connected, OpenClaw validation, owner review, then YouTube test package.
- Block title-thumbnail pairs that are not paid off by visible source proof in the first 30 seconds.
- Block public publishing until owner approval.
- Block private/unlisted upload until owner approval, rights ledger, and quality reports pass.

## Optimization Targets

- Primary: valid public watch hours and subscriber conversion.
- Secondary: first 30-second retention, average view duration, average percentage viewed, impressions click-through rate, browse/suggested traffic, city-request comments.
- Review target: no generic AI feel, no slideshow feel, no fake archival proof, no copied visual identity, no unsupported historical claims.

## Repair Policy

When a long-form draft is rejected, the agent must queue one targeted repair:

- `revise_hook` when the first 30 seconds do not create clear stakes.
- `repair_visuals` when images do not match the narration or change without purpose.
- `repair_source_rights` when historical image, stock photo, stock video, archival video, or source-policy approval is unclear or missing.
- `repair_pacing` when visual beats are too slow, too fast, or monotonous.
- `repair_voice` when narration sounds robotic, mispronounced, or low-trust.
- `repair_persona` when James feels generic, over-personal, fake-biographical, or joke-heavy.
- `repair_proof` when the source artifact is not visible or credible enough.
- `repair_metadata` when title, thumbnail, description, chapters, or subscribe CTA do not match the video.
- `repair_thumbnail_packaging` when the three thumbnails are too similar, too generic, unreadable, rights-unclear, or not click-worthy for the target audience.
- `private_info_risk` when any proof footage may expose private data.
- `retire_topic` when the video cannot become specific, original, sourced, and useful.

No rejected long-form video can be resent for owner approval until the repair reason is logged and the long-form quality report passes.
