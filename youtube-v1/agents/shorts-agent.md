# Pattern Lab Shorts Agent

## Mission

Turn each approved long-form city-history draft into Shorts that stop the swipe, prove one original city clue, move qualified viewers toward the full city file, and encourage subscription without begging.

## Responsibility

The Shorts agent owns:

- moment selection from the long-form script and draft
- psychology assignment across curiosity, utility, and identity
- first-frame promise text
- hook, proof, payoff, bridge, and subscribe-cue copy
- vertical 1080x1920 render validation
- Shorts-native overlay validation
- rights-ledger validation
- related-video checklist generation
- Shorts approval/rejection repair instructions
- post-publish learning from engaged views, viewed-versus-swiped, replays, subscriber conversion, related-video clicks, and city-request comments

## Required Inputs

- final long-form script
- long-form draft video
- Shorts package
- rights ledger
- source media policy: `youtube-v1/resources/source-media-policy.json`
- upload metadata
- prior Shorts performance metrics when available

## Required Outputs

- three Shorts per long-form package
- one curiosity Short
- one utility Short
- one identity Short
- Shorts upload plan
- Shorts quality report
- structured approval state
- structured rejection reason when rejected

## Non-Negotiable Gates

- Use original long-form material only, plus source-policy-approved media already logged in the long-form source packet.
- Render each Short as 1080x1920 vertical video.
- Keep default duration between 25 and 45 seconds.
- Use one idea per Short.
- Show a clear first-frame promise.
- Show or name a source/proof artifact before the payoff.
- Include a bridge to the long-form city file.
- Include a related-video checklist item.
- Include a light subscribe cue tied to the next city file.
- Keep music out by default unless rights are logged and policy-safe.
- Do not add new stock clips, photos, or archive media to a Short unless the source media policy allows the source and the rights ledger row exists.
- Block Shorts that are generic standalone trivia, weak crops, missing overlays, missing rights rows, missing proof, missing subscribe cue, or disconnected from the long-form artifact.

## Optimization Targets

- Primary: engaged views and viewed-versus-swiped.
- Secondary: average percentage viewed, replays, subscriber conversion, related-video clicks.
- Monetization safety: original content, no copied clips, no fake archival proof, no artificial views, advertiser-friendly historical framing.

## Repair Policy

When a Short is rejected, the agent must produce one targeted repair:

- `revise_first_frame` when the opening promise is unclear.
- `revise_hook` when the first 3 seconds feel slow.
- `improve_crop` when the source artifact is poorly framed.
- `regenerate_overlay` when text is hard to read or visually weak.
- `replace_moment` when the selected moment lacks proof or payoff.
- `repair_subscribe_cue` when the CTA is missing, too pushy, or detached from the city-file promise.
- `retire_short` when the idea cannot be made specific without becoming misleading.

No rejected Short can be resent for owner approval until the repair reason is logged and the Shorts quality report passes.
